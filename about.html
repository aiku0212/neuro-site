About this site

Neuroscope is an independent educational project on neuroplasticity and on how the same drugs act across species. It is not a clinical resource.

Not medical or veterinary advice

Nothing here is a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation or dosing guidance. Do not start, stop or change a medication, for yourself or an animal, because of something you read here. Talk to a physician, pharmacist or veterinarian.

Do not give a pet a medication prescribed for a person unless a veterinarian tells you to.

Some drugs described here are controlled or illegal. Explaining a mechanism does not mean a drug is safe, legal or effective.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number. For an animal, call a veterinarian or an animal poison control service.

How far the claims have been checked

Each source's DOI was confirmed with Crossref. The figures on the Evidence and Comparative pages were then compared with their sources, and errors were corrected. That check has limits:

  • Most papers were compared by abstract, not full text.
  • The Spike and Time pages, spine dimensions on the Spines page, and most mechanisms on the Pharmacology page have not been checked this way.
  • A few statements still need a primary source, including parts of the ketamine model.
  • The three bridge sections on the Comparative page are hypotheses, not sourced findings.
  • No outside clinician or veterinarian has reviewed this site yet.

What the adverse-event data can show

The Comparative page uses public FDA adverse-event reports through openFDA. These are spontaneous reports. They cannot show that a drug caused an event, and they have no denominator, so they cannot give the rate at which an event happens. Reporting also varies by drug, species and time, so a higher share of reports is not a higher risk.

This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FDA.

What the interactive models are

The neuron, spine, spike, synapse and time-cell views are teaching models with chosen parameters. The neuron is generated by a branching program, not traced from a scan. Numbers computed from these models describe the model, not a measured brain.
