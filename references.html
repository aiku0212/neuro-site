Where the citations are weakest

The pharmacology page describes stimulant, opioid, benzodiazepine and cannabinoid mechanisms without primary citations. Those mechanisms are settled and appear in every pharmacology textbook, but "textbook consensus" is not a reference, and that section is the least defensible part of the site.

Fixing it means citing the original characterisation papers for each receptor and transporter. That work has not been done yet, and until it is, treat the psychedelic and plasticity sections as the rigorous ones.
