Where the citations are weakest

Each drug class on the pharmacology page now cites a primary paper or review, and lists its main harms. Some harm sources have no DOI, the permanent ID given to a published paper. These are FDA labels, FDA safety communications and pages from the CDC, NIDA and DEA, and they are linked on that page, not listed here. Most of the mechanism papers are rat, mouse or cell studies, and each mechanism card was compared with its source's abstract, not the full text.

Several statements are models, not settled facts. Ketamine's antidepressant mechanism is contested, and the site shows the leading model alongside a competing one.

How psychedelics increase plasticity is also disputed. Moliner et al. report that psychedelics increase plasticity without needing the serotonin 2A receptor (5-HT2A), while Vargas et al. and Shao et al. 2025 find that 5-HT2A is necessary. Most of the cellular evidence comes from mice and cell models, not people.
