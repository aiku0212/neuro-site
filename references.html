Where the citations are weakest

Each drug class on the pharmacology page now cites a primary paper or review. Most of those papers are rat, mouse or cell studies, and each card was compared with its source's abstract, not the full text.

Several statements are models, not settled facts. Ketamine's antidepressant mechanism is contested, and the site shows the leading model alongside a competing one.

Even there, the mechanism is disputed: Moliner et al. report psychedelic plasticity independent of 5-HT2A, while Vargas et al. and Shao et al. 2025 find 5-HT2A necessary. Most of the cellular evidence comes from mice and cell models, not people.
