How to read the labels

Review article
Experts sum up many earlier studies on one topic. It is a good place to start.
Research paper (eLife reviewed preprint)
This reports one new study. eLife publishes it with its peer reviewers' public comments, which can point out weak spots.
University news office
A university writes about its own researchers. These stories are written to promote the work, so read the paper they describe when you can.
